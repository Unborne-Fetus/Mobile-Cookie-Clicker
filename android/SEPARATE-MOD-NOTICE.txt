ANDROID BUILDER / STANDALONE MOD — v1.7
======================================
The standalone Wooden Panel Resizer remains independently installable on PC.
The Android builder now AUTO-PACKAGES its included copy on every build,
without requiring installation in Steam. This behavior was explicitly
requested; it supersedes the previous opt-in version.

The builder also snapshots your other installed Steam mods. It skips older
resizer copies with the same mod ID to avoid duplicate loading.

Your game assets and unrelated Steam mods are never inside the downloaded
builder ZIP. They are copied locally when you run START-HERE.bat.
